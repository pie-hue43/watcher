FAQ

Questions people ask.

Live Listings Monitor

How quickly will I see new listings?

watchr checks each set of preferences about once a minute. A match shows up in your Live Monitor right after that, without reloading.

Do I need to keep the Live Monitor open?

watchr keeps watching in the background. Matches found while the monitor is closed are saved and appear when you open it again, and in Missed Flips.

Can I get alerts on my phone?

Open the Live Monitor in your phone's browser and turn on alerts. Browser support for notifications varies by phone and browser.

Individual Preferences

Which hashtags can I use?

Any word becomes a keyword, like #nike or #dunk. Add #size43 or #sizeM for the size, #min20 and #max80 for the price range, and #newtags, #new, #verygood or #good for the minimum condition.

How many preferences can I add?

As many as you like. Each one is checked separately, so a few focused preferences find matches faster than many broad ones.

Missed Flips

What counts as missed?

A listing watchr found that you never opened from your Live Monitor.

Does it show profit?

No. Missed Flips shows the asking price when watchr found the listing. watchr doesn't know whether or for how much an item sold.

Vinted

Does watchr buy items for me?

No. watchr finds the listings. You decide and buy on Vinted yourself.

Is watchr connected to Vinted?

No. watchr is an independent project. It reads Vinted's public search like a visitor would and keeps its requests sparse. Vinted's terms don't provide for automated searching, so use watchr for your own purchases and in moderation.
