FAQ

Live Sniper

How quickly will I see new listings?

watchr checks each set of preferences about once a minute. A match shows up in Live Sniper right after that, without reloading.

Do I need to keep Live Sniper open?

watchr keeps watching in the background. Matches found while the monitor is closed are saved and appear when you open it again, and in Flips.

Can I get alerts on my phone?

Open Live Sniper in your phone's browser and turn on alerts. Browser support for notifications varies by phone and browser.

Individual Preferences

Which hashtags can I use?

Any word becomes a keyword, like #nike or #dunk. Add #size43 or #sizeM for the size, #min20 and #max80 for the price range, and #newtags, #new, #verygood or #good for the minimum condition. #archive switches on Designer & Archive mode. watchr checks every hashtag against each listing (title, brand, size and condition) and only shows listings that fit all of them. English and German words both work, so #jacket also finds “Jacke”. Category hashtags cover a whole group: #accessories finds belts, jewellery, bags, sunglasses, caps and more, #tops finds shirts, polos, hoodies and sweaters.

How many preferences can I add?

As many as you like. Each one is checked separately, so a few focused preferences find matches faster than many broad ones.

AI Tools

Which AI Tools work without setup?

Resale Check, Niche Radar, Flip Finder, Offer Sniper, Wardrobe Scan, Relist and Sold Alerts run on watchr's own Vinted data. Listing Writer, Smart Search and the written Wardrobe Scan analysis use Claude once an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is set on the server, and fall back to simple rules without it. Studio Shot removes backgrounds with a REMOVE_BG_API_KEY and otherwise improves light, contrast and framing.

Can I repost other people's listings?

No. Relist is meant for your own listings only. Copying other sellers' photos or texts breaks Vinted's rules and their copyright.

Designer & Archive

What does #archive do?

watchr searches through a curated list of 40+ designers by itself, a few per round, and scores every new listing from 0 to 100. Points come from the designer, from signals in the title like archive, runway, sample, AW02 or 90s, and from a price far below the resale estimate. Only pieces scoring 50 or more reach your Live Sniper. Add keywords like #jacket or a #max300 to narrow it down.

How is the resale estimate calculated?

watchr looks up comparable listings on Vinted, with the same brand and the same type of item (for example Ralph Lauren polos), removes the cheapest and most expensive 15 % and takes the median. The value is kept for 24 hours, so frequent items are always priced the same way. It's based on asking prices, not on what items actually sold for, so treat it as a guide.

Why does a listing have no resale estimate?

watchr needs at least five comparable listings and a recognisable brand. Rare pieces sometimes have too few comparisons, and then watchr shows nothing rather than a guess.

Flips

What counts as missed?

A listing watchr found that you never opened from Live Sniper.

Does it show profit?

It shows an estimate. Every find gets an expected net profit: the typical resale price minus 10% for offers, minus the price, buyer protection and shipping. watchr also notices when a find sells on Vinted and ranks those under Flips. Vinted doesn't show the final sale price, so all profit figures are estimates from asking prices. Your own real profit is in My Charts, from the sales you log there.

Vinted

Does watchr buy items for me?

No. watchr finds the listings. You decide and buy on Vinted yourself.

Is watchr connected to Vinted?

No. watchr is an independent project. It reads Vinted's public search like a visitor would and keeps its requests sparse. Vinted's terms don't provide for automated searching, so use watchr for your own purchases and in moderation.
