Features

One live monitor.Everything it needs around it.

watchr is built around the Live Listings Monitor. Every other feature exists to get the right listings into it faster and to help you act on them.

Live

Live Listings Monitor

The heart of watchr. Matches are pushed to your browser over a live connection the moment watchr finds them.

  • New matches slide in at the top, highlighted for a few seconds
  • Photos, price, size and brand in one row
  • Estimated resale price and the difference to the Vinted price on every listing
  • Today at a glance: matches, active preferences, unopened listings
  • Reconnects on its own if your connection drops

Designer & Archive

Specialised in high fashion and archive pieces.

With #archive, watchr rotates through a curated list of designers, from Raf Simons, Helmut Lang and Rick Owens to Margiela, Comme des Garçons, Undercover and Vivienne Westwood, and scores every new listing.

  • Designer rank: archive houses score highest, luxury and premium labels less
  • Title signals: archive, runway, sample, deadstock, season codes like AW02, eras like 90s or Y2K
  • Price bonus when a piece is listed at 60 % of its resale estimate or less
  • Only listings scoring 50 or more make it into your monitor

Resale estimate

For every new listing watchr looks up comparable pieces on Vinted, same brand and same type of item, removes the outliers and takes the median.

  • Shown as “Resale ~€X” with a green or red difference
  • Remembered per product for 24 hours, so items that turn up often, like Ralph Lauren polos, always get about the same value
  • Based on asking prices, so it's a guide, not a guaranteed sale price

Up to 3 photos

watchr fetches up to three photos for each new match, so you can judge an item without opening it.

Browser alerts

Turn on alerts once and get a notification for every new match, even with the tab in the background.

Snipes

A ledger of everything watchr found, with the listings you never opened marked as missed.

No duplicates

Every listing is stored once by its Vinted ID and never reported twice.

Polite by design

One check per minute per preference with random spacing, and an automatic pause when Vinted asks it to slow down.

Snipe categories

Start from Archive, sneakers, jackets, pants, tops or bags with one click and refine with hashtags.

For your website

Put the live feed on your own site.

watchr pushes every new match over a WebSocket. A few lines of JavaScript are enough to show matches anywhere you like.

const ws = new WebSocket("wss://your-watchr-server/ws");
ws.onmessage = (e) => {
  const msg = JSON.parse(e.data);
  if (msg.type === "hit") {
    const h = msg.hit;
    console.log(h.title, h.price, h.resaleEstimate, h.archiveScore, h.url);
  }
};
