How it works

From hashtag to flip.Four steps, fully automatic.

You say what you want once. watchr does the searching, checking and pricing around the clock and only taps you on the shoulder when something is worth it.

Snipe now

  1. Tell watchr what you want

    Type hashtags in Live Sniper, like #archive #max400 or #ralph #lauren #polo #sizeL. Or pick a snipe category and refine it. Add as many sets as you like.

  2. watchr searches Vinted for you

    About once a minute per preference, watchr looks at the newest Vinted listings. With #archive it rotates through 40+ designers on its own. Every listing is stored once, so you never see it twice.

  3. The snipebot checks every listing

    Each hashtag is compared with the title, brand, size and condition, in English and German. Then watchr estimates the resale price from comparable listings and gives archive pieces a score from 0 to 100. Only what fits gets through.

  4. You strike first and track your flips

    A match pops into Live Sniper with photos, price, resale estimate and the difference, plus a browser alert. Open it and decide on Vinted yourself. Everything lands in Flips, so you see what you caught and what you missed.
