FAQ

How quickly will I see new listings?

watchr checks your preferences about once a minute. New matches appear in your dashboard right after that.

Which hashtags can I use?

Any word becomes a keyword, like #nike or #dunk. Add #size43 or #sizeM for the size, #min20 and #max80 for the price range, and #newtags, #new, #verygood or #good for the minimum condition.

Does watchr buy items for me?

No. watchr finds the listings. You decide and buy on Vinted yourself.
