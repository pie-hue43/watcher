Resale estimate

Next to every Vinted price you see what comparable pieces usually go for, and the difference.

  • Median of comparable listings of the same brand and type, outliers removed
  • Remembered per product for 24 hours, so a Ralph Lauren polo is always priced about the same
  • An estimate from asking prices, not a guaranteed sale price
