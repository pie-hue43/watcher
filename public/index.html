    FAQ

    Questions people ask.

    How quickly will I see new listings?

    Usually within about a minute. watchr checks each set of preferences roughly once a minute and a match shows up in Live Sniper right after that, without reloading. With many preferences a round can take a little longer.

    Which hashtags can I use?

    Any word becomes a keyword, like #nike or #dunk. Add #size43 or #sizeM for the size, #min20 and #max80 for the price range, and #newtags, #new, #verygood or #good for the minimum condition. #archive switches on Designer & Archive mode. watchr checks every hashtag against each listing (title, brand, size and condition) and only shows listings that fit all of them. English and German words both work, so #jacket also finds “Jacke”. Category hashtags cover a whole group: #accessories finds belts, jewellery, bags, sunglasses, caps and more, #tops finds shirts, polos, hoodies and sweaters.

    How is the resale estimate calculated?

    watchr looks up comparable listings on Vinted (same brand, same type of item, for example Ralph Lauren polos), removes outliers and takes the median. That value is kept for 24 hours, so frequent items are always priced the same way. It's based on asking prices, so treat it as a guide, not a promise.

    What are Flips?

    Everything watchr found for you, marked by whether you opened it. watchr keeps checking your finds and ranks the ones that sold by estimated net profit. Vinted doesn't show final sale prices, so the profit is an estimate from asking prices.

    Does watchr buy items for me?

    No. watchr finds the listings. You decide and buy on Vinted yourself.
